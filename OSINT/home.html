OSINT Challenges

Sharpen your investigative skills with our series of challenges. Challenge 1 is live!

  • Challenge 1: Digital Footprint (Released)

    Your first mission is to analyze the digital footprint of a target. You've been given a single piece of information: a username, "cipher_sleuth". Your objective is to find out as much public information as you can about this user. This includes finding social media profiles, identifying other usernames they might use, and locating any publicly available data related to them.

  • Challenge 2: Geolocation (Upcoming)

  • Challenge 3: Metadata Mayhem (Upcoming)

    Extract hidden metadata from a file to uncover critical information about its origin and creator.

  • Challenge 4: Reverse Image Search (Upcoming)

    Trace an image back to its original source to expose a disinformation campaign.

  • Challenge 5: The Dark Web Dive (Upcoming)

  • Challenge 6: Social Engineering Recon (Upcoming)

  • Challenge 7: The Email Trail (Upcoming)

    Given an email address, find a wealth of information about the user, including their connected accounts and professional history.

  • Challenge 8: Public Records Pursuit (Upcoming)

  • Challenge 9: API Exploitation (Upcoming)

  • Challenge 10: Financial Footprints (Upcoming)

  • Challenge 11: OSINT and AI (Upcoming)

  • Challenge 16: Linguistic Analysis (Upcoming)

  • Challenge 17: OSINT for Journalism (Upcoming)

  • Challenge 18: Threat Intelligence (Upcoming)

  • Challenge 19: The Time Traveler (Upcoming)

  • Challenge 20: The Ultimate OSINT Test (Upcoming)
