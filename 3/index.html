// 0x01. SYSTEM INFO // ABOUT BSIDES INDORE

B-Sides Indore is a non-profit, community-driven framework for organizing and holding open, low-cost security conferences. Every B-Sides is a unique event with its own theme, style, and content. It's an opportunity for individuals to present their research, engage with the local community, and discuss the "next big things" in information security.

**MISSION LOG:** To create a platform for the passionate minds of Central India to connect, share knowledge, and push the boundaries of cyber defense and offense.
